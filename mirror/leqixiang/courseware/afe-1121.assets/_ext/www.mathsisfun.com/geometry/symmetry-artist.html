Symmetry Artist

Mathematics and Art come together!

images/symmetry-artist.js

Draw on the canvas and watch your art repeat in beautiful, symmetric patterns.

You can explore reflection symmetry (mirror images) and rotational symmetry (spinning patterns) as you go.

You can also nudge the most recent addition by using the up down left right keys.
Press ctrl to make smaller adjustments.
