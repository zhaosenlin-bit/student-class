Balancing Act Simulation: Torque and Rotational Equilibrium

This interactive 3D physics simulation demonstrates how mass, force, and lever arm affect torque and rotational equilibrium.

Torque and Rotational Direction

Torque describes the turning effect of a force about a pivot. In the standard sign convention, counterclockwise torque is positive and clockwise torque is negative.

τ = r × F

Rotational Equilibrium

A beam is in rotational equilibrium when the sum of all torques about its pivot is zero.

Στ = 0

How to Use the Simulation

  • Adjust the positions and masses of the objects.
  • Use the +/− buttons for precise adjustments.
  • Observe clockwise and counterclockwise rotation.
  • Use Three Objects mode to investigate multiple torques.
  • Compare individual torques with the net torque.
