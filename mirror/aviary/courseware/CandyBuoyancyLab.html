Candy Buoyancy Lab
You will be floating a container in a tank full of a fluid. You will be looking for the relationship between the volume submerged and the mass of the candy in the container. Your job is to find an equation that will predict the volume submerged as a function of the mass of candy present.

Once you develop your equation, you will use it to find the volume submerged with no candy and the number of grams required to sink the container by 1 mL.
