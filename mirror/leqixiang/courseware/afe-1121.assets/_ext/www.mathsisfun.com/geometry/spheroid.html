Ellipsoid

An ellipsoid is the more general case of a spheroid.

  • A spheroid is a specific type of ellipsoid that's symmetric about its polar axis (like a squashed or stretched sphere)
  • An ellipsoid is the general 3D shape where all three perpendicular axes can have different lengths
