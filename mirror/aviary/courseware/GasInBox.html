Gas in Box Lab
In this lab we will be looking a sample of a gas. You can set the walls of the container to be conductive or insulated. You can set the gas to be any of the noble gases. You can lock the piston in place, allow it to adjust to changes in pressure, or manual move it forward or backward. You can put the container in contact with 8 different environments with different temperatures.

You can measure the volume of the container, the pressure of the gas, the temperature of the gas, the number of moles in the container and the average speed of the molecules. You will also be told how much work has been done and how much heat has entered or left the container
