The Way a Mirror Works Lab (Convex)
In this program you will be modeling the behavior of a mirror by having Aubrie fire hockey pucks into boards with different radii of curvature. The behavior of the pucks coming off the boards will mimic the way light will bounce off a mirror of the same shape as the boards.

You will be able to change the radius of curvature of the boards. The X on the principle axis shows the center of curvature of the mirror.

When you are ready to start the experiment, click on the begin button.
