Vertical Motion of Firework Lab
In this lab you will be firing fireworks straight up from the ground. You will have control over the launch speed of the firework and the fuse time that will determine when the firework will explode. You are to see how each parameter affects the height of explosion and the speed at the time of the explosion.

Click on the launch tube in the bottom right of the screen when you are ready to launch your firework or reset your system.

When you are ready to start the problem, click on the Begin button and when you have finished hit End to submit your results.
