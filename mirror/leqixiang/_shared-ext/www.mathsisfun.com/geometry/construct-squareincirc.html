Square Inscribed in a Circle

How to construct a square inscribed in circle
using just a compass and a straightedge

images/construct.js?mode=squareincirc

Why It Works

The two diameters cross at 90° and bisect (cut in half) each other at the circle's center.

This divides the circle into four equal 90° arcs. Connecting the points gives four equal sides and four 90° corners, forming a square.
