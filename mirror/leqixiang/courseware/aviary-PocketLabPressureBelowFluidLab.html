PocketLab Pressure Below a Fluid Lab
In this activity, you will investigate the connection between the depth below a fluid and the pressure experienced at that location. This experiment will utilize a PocketLab protected by flexible plastic.

When measuring depth, measure to the top of the PocketLab.
