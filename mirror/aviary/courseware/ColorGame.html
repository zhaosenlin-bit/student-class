Color Matching Game
For this activity you will be presented with a series of five randomly selected color samples. For each of the color samples you are given, you need to try to break this sample down into its primary color constituents. You will be able to independently adjust the amounts of red, green and blue to create the color sample you are trying to match.

You will be points based on how closely your color matches with the color sample you were presented. When you are ready to start, enter your name and hit "Begin"
