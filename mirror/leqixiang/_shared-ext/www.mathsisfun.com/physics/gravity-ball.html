Ball Physics Animation

Click, drag, release

images/gravity-ball.js

Give it a try: Click, drag and release a ball high up. What happens when a heavy ball hits a lighter one? What happens when you increase the fluid density?

This is a mathematical model of ball physics that includes:

  • momentum and collisions
  • gravity
  • mass
  • drag from air (1), water (1000) or denser fluids (up to 10000)

It isn't super accurate, as it only does the calculations about 25 times a second ... so it can "jitter" when objects are packed closely, or let objects overlap when gravity or density is high. But otherwise it is pretty cool!
