Live Acceleration in Circle Lab
In this activity, you will investigate the connection between speed of your circular motion and the acceleration experienced by your phone. This lab can only be completed using a phone with built in accelerometers. You will be asked for permission before this lab will work.

In this lab you will need to place your phone horizontally so that the screen is facing toward the ceiling.

You will also need to point the arrow at the top of the screen away from the center of the circle.

When you are ready to start this activity, click on the begin button.
