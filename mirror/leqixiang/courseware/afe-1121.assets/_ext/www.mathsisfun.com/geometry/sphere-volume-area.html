How to Calculate the Volume and Surface Area

Surface Area = 4 × π × r2

Example: r = 5

Surface Area = 4 × π × r2= 4 × π × 52= 4 × π × 25= 100 π≈ 314

Volume = (4/3) × π × r3

Example: r = 5

Volume = (4/3) × π × r3 = (4/3) × π × 53= (4/3) × π × 125≈ 524
