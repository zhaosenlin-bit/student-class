2D Shapes

A 2D (plane) shape has 2 dimensions (such as width and height) but no thickness.

A piece of paper feels flat, but it still has a tiny bit of thickness. A true 2D shape is perfectly flat and has absolutely no thickness.

Regular Polygons

A polygon is a 2D shape with straight sides.
A regular polygon has all sides equal and all angles equal:


Triangle - 3 Sides
Square - 4 Sides

Pentagon - 5 Sides
Hexagon - 6 sides

Heptagon - 7 Sides
Octagon - 8 Sides

Nonagon - 9 Sides
Decagon - 10 Sides
More ...

Irregular Polygons

These are 2D shapes, and they are polygons, but irregular because their sides or angles aren't all equal:


Quadrilateral
Any 4 sided 2D shape
Rectangle - 4 Sides
All right angles
And many more!

Curved Shapes

These are also 2D shapes that are not polygons because they have curves:


Circle
Ellipse
And many more!
