Father Brown Acceleration Lab
Father Brown has a new electric bike that will help him get around Kembleford with greater efficiency. In this activity, he is testing out its acceleration by seeing how much time it takes to cover the ground between to markers while accelerating at a constant rate.

Once you develop a model of how the distance between the markers affects the time to accelerate that distance, you will make predictions based on that model.
