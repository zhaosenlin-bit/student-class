Clocks at Different Distances from the Sun

Stationary clocks closer to the Sun tick more slowly than a clock far away.

Clock rates: dτ/dt = √(1 − 2GM/rc²). Stationary clocks outside a spherical, nonrotating Sun; orbital motion is not included. The dial hands are illustrative, while the numerical differences are calculated.
