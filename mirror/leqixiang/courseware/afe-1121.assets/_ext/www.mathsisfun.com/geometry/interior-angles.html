Interior Angle

An Interior Angle is an angle inside a shape

Here's another example:

When we add up the Interior Angle and its corresponding Exterior Angle we get a Straight Angle (180°), making them "Supplementary Angles".

 

Interior angles appear in all polygons, from triangles to complex shapes.

The total of a shape’s interior angles depends on its number of sides.
