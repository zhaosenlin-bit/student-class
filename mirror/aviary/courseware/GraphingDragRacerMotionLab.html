Two-Stage Motion Graphing
This lab will let you create position vs. time graphs for Allie's Drag Racer as it rushes down the 400 m track. You will be able to change some of the parameters of the situation and then collect data for the motion of the racer down the track. The car will accelerate for the first part of the race and then move at a constant speed for the remainder of the race
