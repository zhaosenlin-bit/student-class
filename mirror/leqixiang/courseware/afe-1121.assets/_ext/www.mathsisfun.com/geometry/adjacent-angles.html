Adjacent Angles

Two angles are adjacent when they share a common side and a common vertex (corner point), and don't overlap.

Angle ABC is adjacent to angle CBD

Because:

  • they have a common side (CB)
  • they have a common vertex (point B)
  • they don't overlap

What's and Isn't an Adjacent Angle

Adjacent Angles

they share a vertex and a side

NOT Adjacent Angles

they only share a vertex, not a side

NOT Adjacent Angles

they only share a side,
not a vertex

NOT Adjacent Angles

angles a and b overlap

Example: Clock Hands

Clock hands can often form adjacent angles between them, but can you think of times they won't?

Summary

Two angles are adjacent when they:

  • share a common side
  • share a common vertex
  • don't overlap
