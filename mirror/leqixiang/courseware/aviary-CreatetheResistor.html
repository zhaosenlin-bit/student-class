Resistor Creation Challenge
This program will make sure you can correctly create a resistor with the correct color bands to give you the resistance that you desire. You will be told the resistance that you need and you will need to specify the color combination that would go with that resistance. You will have three minutes to correctly specify as many resistors as possible. Wrong answers will reset your score back to 0.

When you are ready to start this activity, click on the begin button.
