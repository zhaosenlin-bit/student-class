Instantaneous Velocity Graph Maker
This program is designed for teachers to make a position vs. time graph that you can print out for students to use on a test, quiz, or worksheet

One of the things you could have the students do is find the instantaneous velocity at some point along the graph

Toggle on and off the tangent line to have a graph for your answer key or for your students
