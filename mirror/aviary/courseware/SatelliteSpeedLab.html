Satellite Speed Lab
In this lab you will be looking the connection between the radius of a satellite's orbit and the orbital speed of the satellite. Once you have established a relationship, you will use that relationship to make predictions on things you have not measured.

The size of the satellites are greatly exaggerated to make them visible. You planet may look like Earth, but the mass of your planet has been randomly generated.
