PocketLab Freefall Lab
In this activity, you will investigate the connection between the height from which an object is dropped and the time it takes to reach the ground. This experiment will utilize a PocketLab to time the free fall and it will not take place on Earth.

When measuring height, measure to the bottom of the bucket.
