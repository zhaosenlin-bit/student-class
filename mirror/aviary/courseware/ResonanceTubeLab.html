Resonance Tube Lab
In this lab you will be looking at standing waves that are created in wind instruments. When you load the program you will have 5 random length tubes generated for you. You can use the tube with both ends open or with one end closed. You should be able to find fundamental frequency and at least one higher harmonic for each tube.

When you are ready to start the experiment, click on the begin button.
