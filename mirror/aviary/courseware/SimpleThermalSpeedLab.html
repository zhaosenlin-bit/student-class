Thermal Speed Lab
In this lab we will be looking at the factors that affect the average speed of a gas particle. You will have control over the mass of the particles in the box and the temperature of the gas in the box.

Take your readings on the most sensitive speed scale possible. Don't take readings if the meter is pinned or close to zero.
