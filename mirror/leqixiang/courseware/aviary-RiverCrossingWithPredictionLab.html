River Crossing With Prediction Lab
In this activity you will determine the relationship between the speed setting on your boat and the distance it drifts down river before reaching the opposite shore. Once you have determined your relationship, you will use that to make predictions on things you haven't tested.

Try not to let your boat get lost or damaged as it travels across the water.
