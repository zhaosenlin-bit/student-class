Volume

Volume is the amount of 3-dimensional space something takes up.

Imagine how much water could be in it.

Also called Capacity.

Change the volume using the slider below:

images/volume-demo.js?mode=mix

Units of volume include:

  • Metric: cubic centimeters (cm3), cubic meters (m3), liters
  • US Standard: fluid ounce, cubic inch, cubic foot, pints, gallons

Example: measured in meters (m):

The volume of this cuboid is 10 m × 4 m × 5 m = 200 m3

Volumes of Common 3D Shapes

The volume of a Sphere:


Volume = 4 3 × π × r3

Also see Volume and Area of a Sphere.

The volume of a Cone:


Volume = 1 3 π × r2 × h

The volume of a Cylinder:


Volume = π × r2 × h
