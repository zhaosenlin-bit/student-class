Guided Energy Loss Lab
The purpose of this lab is to look at the energy lost for a ball when it bounces. We will be looking at how much energy is lost when the ball is dropped from differing heights.

This activity will lead you step by step through the procedure. First, you will collect the data virtually as you go through this procedure and then you will graph it. Then if you data comes out good, you will get a certificate that you can share with your teacher.

Once you get your certificate you should go through this procedure again, but with real equipment.
