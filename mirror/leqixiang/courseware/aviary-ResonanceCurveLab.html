Resonance Curve Lab
In this lab you will be looking at the effect of resistance on the shape of the resonance curve for an RLC circuit. You will be able to change the frequency and quickly collect data on the peak voltage across the resistor for your circuit.

You can also vary the capacitance and inductance of your circuit to see how they affect the peak.

Clicking on the maximum frequency on your graph will change the scale on the x-axis.

Click begin to start your experiment.
