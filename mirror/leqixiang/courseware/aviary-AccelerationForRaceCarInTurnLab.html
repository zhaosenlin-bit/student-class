Acceleration in a Turn Lab
In this activity, you will investigate the connection between speed at which a car goes around a circle and the acceleration felt by the driver. This experiment will utilize a PocketLab placed in the center of the car to determine the acceleration caused by car speeding through the turn.

This experiment should not be conducted in real life.
