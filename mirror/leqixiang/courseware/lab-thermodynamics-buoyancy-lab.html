Overflow Volume and Archimedes' Principle

This interactive 3D physics simulation demonstrates how the volume of water overflowing from a full container can be used to measure the volume of a submerged object. It also shows the special case of a floating object, where only part of the object is submerged.

How to Use

  • Select an object shape.
  • Change the object size scale.
  • Click Put Object in Water.
  • Compare the expected overflow volume with the measured overflow volume.

What Students Learn

  • Submerged Objects

    For objects that sink, the displaced water volume is equal to the object's full volume.

  • Floating Objects

    For objects that float, only the submerged part displaces water. The displaced water weight equals the object's weight.

  • Density and Floating

    Objects with density lower than water float, while objects with density greater than water sink.
