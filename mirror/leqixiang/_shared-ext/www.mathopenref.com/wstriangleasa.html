Construct a triangle given one side and two adjacent angles with compass and straightedge
(For assistance see www.mathopenref.com/consttriangleasa.html)
1. Construct a triangle which has one side equal to the given line segment and where the angles at each end of that segment are equal in measure to the angles M and N.
2. Construct a triangle which has one side equal to the given line segment and where the angles at each end of that segment are equal in measure to the angles F and G. Explain any problem you may have with the construction. (For a hint see "Interior Angles of a Triangle" at mathopenref.com/triangleinternalangles.html
