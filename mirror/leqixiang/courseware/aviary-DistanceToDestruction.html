Distance to Destruction Lab
In this lab you will be looking at how the distance between two speakers determines the type of interference created by two synchronized waves. In this program, the sine curves you are viewing represent fluctuations in pressure caused by the vibrating speakers (sound waves).

Use the arrows above the waves to move the location of speaker 2. The speed of the waves have been visually slowed down to make the behaviour easier to see.

When you are ready to start the experiment, click on the begin button.
