Any Given Sunday Lab
It has been said that on any given Sunday* any football team can beat any other football team. In this lab we are going to pretend that the odds of winning any particular game are 50/50. You are going to run a quick simulated season and see what the record of your team turns out to be.

As a class we will have a goal of running a total of 50 simulated seasons and we will look at the frequency of different final records for each season.

When you are ready to start playing your fantasy football, hit the begin button.
*Over the years, Sunday has come to mean Sunday, Monday Night, Late Monday Night, Thursday Night, and the occasional $aturday afternoon. Now it seems to also mean Tuesday when teams don't do what they are supposed to do.
