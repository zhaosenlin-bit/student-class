Thermal Speed Lab with Predictions
In this program you will looking at the connection between the temperature of a sample of a noble gas and the average speed of the particles that make up the gas. Remember that a sample will be composed of particles with all different speeds and we are looking only at the average speed.

Once you have determined the relationships, you will be making predictions based on your results.
