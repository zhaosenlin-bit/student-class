Graph Skills Challenge
In this activity you will be given a data collection strategy and you must walk through the steps necessary to create a good graph for determining the relationship for the data you collected.

If you get any of the steps wrong along the way, you will need to repeat the entire activity again.

When you are ready to start the challenge, click on the Begin button. When you have successfully completed all the steps, you will be given a completion certificate.
