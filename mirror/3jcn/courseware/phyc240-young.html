About Young's Double-Slit Experiment

Light passing through two narrow slits produces two coherent waves. The waves overlap and create an interference pattern of bright and dark fringes on the observation screen.

  • Bright fringes (constructive interference): path difference ≈ mλ.
  • Dark fringes (destructive interference): path difference ≈ (m + ½)λ.
  • Wavelength: increasing λ makes fringes farther apart.
  • Slit separation: increasing d brings fringes closer together.
  • Screen distance: increasing L makes fringes farther apart.

Click Start Simulation to animate wavefronts from the light source and the two slits. Use the sliders to change the calculated fringe pattern in real time. Click Hide Waves or Hide Graph to focus on a particular feature; use Pause and Reset to control the animation.
