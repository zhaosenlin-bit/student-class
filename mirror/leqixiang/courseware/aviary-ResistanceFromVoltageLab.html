Resistance From Voltage Lab With Predictions
For this lab you will be looking at how the radius of a wire affects the resistance of a wire.

To find the resistance we will measure the voltage required to create a current of 1 amp through our wire.

Once we have established our model, we will use it to make predictions on things we haven't tested.
