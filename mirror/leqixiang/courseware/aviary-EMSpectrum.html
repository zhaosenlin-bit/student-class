EM Spectrum Lab
In this lab you will be examining the spectrum that is produced when you are viewing a white light filament through a diffraction grating. You will have access to many different gratings and you will be able to move the location of the filament closer or farther from the grating

Take note of how the different parameters affect how far the colors appear to be from the filament
