RC Series Circuit Visualizer (Graphs)
This program is designed to help students graphically visualize the changes that take place to the voltages in an RC series circuit as time progresses

You will be able to cycle through different values of Resistance, Capacitance, and Voltage

Time has been slowed down so you get a better feel for what is happening
