Power of Schrödinger’s Equation

Solutions of Schrödinger’s equation explain the shapes of hydrogen orbitals.
Schrödinger Eq & Hydrogen Orbitals
Time-independent Schrödinger equation
Ĥψ = Eψ
[ -ħ2/(2μ) ∇2 - e2/(4πϵ0r) ] ψ(r, θ, φ) = E ψ(r, θ, φ)
Hydrogen-atom solution
ψnℓm(r, θ, φ) = Rnℓ(r) Yℓm(θ, φ)
Rnℓ(r): radial part → size and radial structure
Yℓm(θ, φ): angular part → shape and orientation
Quantum numbers
n = principal quantum number → energy level and size
ℓ = angular quantum number → orbital type and shape
m = magnetic quantum number → orientation in space
