Sign

A sign is a symbol placed in front of a number to indicate whether it is a positive or negative.

A plus sign ( + ) is used to indicate a positive number. Positive numbers are considered to be greater than zero. If the sign is missing it is considered to be positive. So the numbers 45 and +45 are identical.

A minus sign ( – ) indicates that the number is negative. Negative numbers are considered to be less than zero. For example -34.

Don't confuse signs with arithmetic operators

One of the great confusions in math is the decision to use the + and – symbols to mean two different things. They are used as signs to indicate positive and negative numbers. But they are also used to mean the add and subtract operations.

To avoid confusion, you should refer to the sign of a number by saying "positive ten" or "negative 23".

When referring to the operations, only then should you say "plus" and "minus" . For example "two plus three equals five".

Just to show how weird this can get, the equation would be read as "negative 7 minus negative 5 plus negative 1 equals positive 11"
