Ground to Ground Soccer Kick Lab
In this problem you will be able to look at how the speed and angle of a kick determine the horizontal displacement of a soccer kick, the maximum height of the kick and the time the kick is in the air. You will also be able to adjust the gravitational field of the planet on which the soccer ball is being kicked.



When you are ready to start this activity, click on the begin button.
