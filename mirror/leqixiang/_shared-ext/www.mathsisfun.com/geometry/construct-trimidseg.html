Triangle Midsegment Construction

How to construct a triangle midsegment
using just a compass and a straightedge

A midsegment of a triangle is a line segment that connects the midpoints of two sides.

images/construct.js?mode=trimidseg

A midsegment has these nice properties:

  • It is parallel to the third side
  • Its length is exactly 12 the length of the third side
