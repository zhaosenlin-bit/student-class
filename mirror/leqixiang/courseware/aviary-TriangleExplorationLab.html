Triangle Exploration Lab
In this program you will be able to separately control the length of the horizontal leg, the vertical leg, and the hypotenuse of your right triangle.

If you select touch version, you must touch and release the arrows to activate/deactivate. In the desktop version you will hold the arrows down for as long as you want it active.

Switch between rectangular view and polar view to measure different properties of your triangle.
