The Way a Mirror Works Lab (Plane)
In this program you will be modeling the behavior of a mirror by having Aubrie fire hockey pucks into boards rotated at different angles. The behavior of the pucks coming off the boards will mimic the way light will bounce off a mirror rotated at the same angle as the boards.

You will be able to change the angle of the boards.

When you are ready to start the experiment, click on the begin button.
