Newton’s Second Law (F = ma)

This interactive 3D physics simulation demonstrates Newton’s Second Law using a cart–pulley system. Adjust mass and friction to explore how acceleration changes in real time.

F = ma

System Equation

a = (m₂g − μm₁g) / (m₁ + m₂)

How to Use

  • Adjust masses
  • Change friction
  • Click Start

What Students Learn

  • Effect of Cart Mass

    When the mass of the cart increases, the acceleration decreases for the same pulling force. A larger total mass makes the system harder to accelerate.

  • Effect of Hanging Mass

    Increasing the hanging mass increases the gravitational force pulling the system, resulting in a greater acceleration.

  • Effect of Friction

    Friction opposes motion. As friction increases, the net force decreases, leading to a smaller acceleration.
