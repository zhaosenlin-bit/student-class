Perimeter of a Square

Definition: The total distance around the outside of a square
Try this Drag any orange dot to resize the square. The perimeter is calculated as you drag.

How to find the perimeter of a square

Like any polygon, the perimeter is the total distance around the outside, which can be found by adding together the length of each side. I the case of a square, all four sides are the same length, so the perimeter is four times the length of a side. Or as a formula: where:
