Circle Center by Two Chords Construction

How to construct a circle center by two chords
using just a compass and a straightedge

images/construct.js?mode=circlecenter2

Steps

  1. Draw any two chords across the circle (but not parallel)
  2. Construct the perpendicular bisector of each chord
  3. Where the two bisector lines cross is the center of the circle!
