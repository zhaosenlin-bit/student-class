Reference Materials

The Physics Lecture-Demonstration Facility Reference Files contain a variety of information for the purpose of aiding teaching using the demonstrations in our file as well as providing background information regarding the demonstrations. Among the materials to be found in these files are:

  • 1. Information regarding the use of the demonstrations described in these documents, including instructions for use and sample data, where appropriate.
  • 2. Information on the theory of the demonstration, including papers discussing the theory as well as information regarding both sides of issues where there is some controversy regarding either the interpretation of the experiment or its explanation.
  • 3. Information regarding the construction of our demonstrations, including parts and materials used, where available, and sketches or mechanical drawings.
  • 4. Extensions of the experiments in our library, including information on similar demonstrations designed to illustrate the same physical principles.
  • 5. Information regarding similar demonstrations, including less expensive (or more expensive) versions of the same demonstrations and other demonstrations which show related concepts.

    These demonstration reference files can be reached through the active list below or through the "REFERENCES" entry for each demonstration description. If they are called from a demonstration description you may brouse through the references in that sub-topic, but when you return using the Netscape back button puts you in the demonstration from which you came.
