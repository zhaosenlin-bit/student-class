Newton's Law Lab With Predictions
In this activity you will be looking at the connection between the mass of a hover puck and the acceleration of the puck. There will be a constant force of unknown value applied to this puck. Through the completion of this activity you will determine the approximate value of this force and you will use the graph equation to make predictions about things you didn't test.
