Horizontal Oscillation Lab
In this lab you will be looking at the different changes that take place for horizontal oscillations when the speed or mass of an object is changed or the spring constant of the spring is varied

Students can use the position vs. time graph to find the amplitude, frequency, period and/or angular frequency of oscillation

The hovercraft will stick to the spring and experience negligible mechanical energy loss upon the collision
