Vector Exploration Lab with Predictions
In this program you will looking at the connection between magnitude of a velocity vector and the x and y components of this vector. You will be able to change the magnitude of the vector and you will be measuring the x and y components for each of the different magnitudes.

Once you have determined the relationships, you will be making predictions based on your results.
