Circular Acceleration
In this program you will be guided through the process of determining the acceleration of an object in uniform circular motion. This program is designed to help you see where the formula for circular acceleration comes from and also understanding the direction of circular acceleration.



When you are ready to start the problem, click on the Begin button and when you have finished hit End to submit your results.
