Friction with Prediction Lab
In this lab you will be looking at the relationship between the force friction and the force normal between two surfaces. In this experiment you will get a box that will be pulled across a table surface at a slow steady speed. You will be able to add mass to the box to change the force normal between the surfaces and you will be able to measure the frictional force by pulling the object across the table at a slow steady speed
