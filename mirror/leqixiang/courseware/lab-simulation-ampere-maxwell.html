Ampère–Maxwell Law (Charging & Discharging Capacitor)

∮B·dl = μ₀(Icond + ε₀ dΦE/dt)

“Surface mode” switches the enclosed current calculation: conduction current in the wire (Icond) or displacement current across the gap (Id). Maxwell’s correction makes both surfaces give the same ∮B·dl.
