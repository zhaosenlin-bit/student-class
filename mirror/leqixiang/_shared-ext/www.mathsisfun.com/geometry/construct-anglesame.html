Same Angle Construction

How to construct a Congruent Angle
using just a compass and a straightedge

Why Does It Work?

This construction secretly builds two identical triangles!

By using the same compass settings, we make:

  • Two sides with matching lengths (the radius of the first arc)
  • A third side with a matching length (the distance between the intersection points)

Because all three sides are equal (Side-Side-Side or SSS), the angles at their vertices must be equal.
