Doppler Lab with Predictions
In this program you will looking at the connection between the speed of an airplane and the wavelength of the waves in front of the plane.

Your plane will be equipped with a very low frequency sound generator for this experiment.

Once you have determined the relationships, you will be making predictions based on your results.
