Electric Field of a Very Large Uniformly Charged Sheet
Surface Charge Density, σ (μC/m²)
4.0 μC/m²
Theory: for an infinite sheet, the field magnitude is constant: |E| = σ / (2ε₀).
Point P Position along z-axis (cm)
25 cm
Direction: for positive σ, the electric field points away from the sheet on both sides.
