Drone Delivery Lab
In this lab you are trying to discover the factors that affect the motion of a small object that is released by a toy drone. You will be able to control the speed and height of the drone as well as the gravitational field where the drone is operating

Be sure to measure height from the bottom of the falling object and to measure horizontal displacement while falling (this is not the same as the landing location)
