Electrical Determination of Specific Heat Lab
In this lab you will be collecting data relating the temperature of a fluid to the time over which electrical energy has been added to the fluid. Based on this data, you are to determine the specific heat of the fluid you are heating up. You can assume that the system is closed and all the energy that enters the fluid turns in to heat and remains in the fluid.
