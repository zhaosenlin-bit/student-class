Circle Center by Right Angles

How to construct a circle center by right angles
using something right-angled (plastic triangle, square, sheet of paper, etc)

images/construct.js?mode=circleright

 

Why? Because when we place a right angle on a circle's edge, then two lines coming from it will intersect the circle exactly opposite each other (forming a diameter).

And where two diameters meet is the center.
