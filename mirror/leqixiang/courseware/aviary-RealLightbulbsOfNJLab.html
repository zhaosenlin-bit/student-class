Real Lightbulbs of NJ
In this experiment you will be looking at how real light bulbs differ from the model we developed when studying ohm's law. You will get a lightbulb that was designed to be used with 12 V but you will be powering it with a variety of voltage lower than the recommended voltage. You can measure the power and/or the current for each of these different voltages.

You can then create graphs to see how they are different than the graphs for a real light bulb.

When you are ready to start, hit the begin button.
