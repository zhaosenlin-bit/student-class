Emission Spectra Lab
In this lab you will be examining the spectrum that is produced when you are viewing gas emissions of different elements through a diffraction grating. You will have access to many different gratings and you will be able to move the location of the filament closer or farther from the grating.

You will be able to cycle through the spectra a few different elements.
