Drone Delivery Challenge
In this challenge you are trying to deliver as many objects as possible to the target that will be located on the ground. The points you earn for each accurately delivered object will increase with greater heights and greater horizontal speeds.

If you get a target that you cannot hit given the speed and height of the drone, stop the drone (click on speed readout) over the target and drop the package for no points.

On the first level you will be dealing with Earth gravity, the second level you will be dealing with the gravity on mars and the last level will simulate gravity on the moon. The craft will be in a pressured environment so it will still be able to fly on the moon. Select the time per round to begin.
