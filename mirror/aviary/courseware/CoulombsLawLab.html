Coulomb's Law Lab
In this program you will be looking at the factors that affect the force between two charged pith balls. You will be determining the force based on the angle to which the pith ball is moved when in the vicinity of the other charged object.

You will be able to change the strength and type of charge. You will also be able to change the distance between the charges.

When you are ready to start the problem, click on the begin button.
