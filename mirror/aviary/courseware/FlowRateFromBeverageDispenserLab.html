Flow Rate From Beverage Dispenser Lab
In this lab you are trying to discover the factors that affect the flow rate from a beverage dispenser. Students will be able to control the height of the water in the dispenser, the gravitational field strength of the planet, the density of the fluid being dispensed, and the size of the spigot from which the fluid is being dispensed
