Flux in Spinning Coil Lab
In this program you are to plot out the magnetic flux as a function of time for a spinning coil of wires. Try to collect data every 10° from 0° all the way to 400°. Then create a spreadsheet of your data showing time, angle and flux. Use your spreadsheet to find the change in flux for each 10° increment and then use the spreadsheet to calculate the electromotive force (Ɛ = -∆Φ/∆t). Plot out graphs of Φ vs t and Ɛ vs t.
